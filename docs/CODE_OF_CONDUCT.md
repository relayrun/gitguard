# Git Guard community guidelines

This community is for constructive discussion of Git Guard, its guidance, translations, examples and related collaboration workflows.

- Be respectful. Critique ideas and behavior without insults, harassment or discrimination.
- Keep discussion relevant and give others room to ask questions or disagree.
- Do not post spam, impersonate others or share another person's private information.
- Use public or invented examples. Remove credentials and private data from reports and attachments.
- Search existing topics and use the category that best fits your question.

Maintainers may move, edit, hide or remove content, close or lock discussions, and restrict participation when needed to keep the community constructive. Use GitHub's report-abuse tools for abusive content; avoid reposting sensitive material in public reports.

These guidelines apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions. Participation does not grant repository write access or guarantee that a suggestion will be implemented.
